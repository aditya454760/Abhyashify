GATE study planner and timetable

Postgraduate engineering and science entrance (M.Tech, IISc, PSUs). Abhyashify loads the GATE subjects, asks when your exam is and when you like to study, then builds a day-by-day timetable up to exam day.

How the timetable is built

Subjects are spread across the days up to the exam date, weighted by importance. Your hours, times of day and days off are respected, a revision buffer and mock tests are kept at the end, and every block can be edited.

Note: the subjects and usual months here were written from general knowledge, not downloaded from official sites. Check the syllabus and dates in the exam body's own notice.

Questions and answers

Which GATE papers does Abhyashify cover?

It covers: CS (Computer Science & IT), DA (Data Science & AI), ECE (Electronics & Communication), EE (Electrical), ME (Mechanical), CE (Civil), CH (Chemical), IN (Instrumentation), BT (Biotechnology), PI (Production & Industrial), XE (Engineering Sciences), XL (Life Sciences). Each one has its subjects preloaded, and you can change them.

When is GATE held?

Usually early February (some years late January). The dates change every year. Usually early February. This is only a hint. Abhyashify never treats it as the real date and asks you to confirm your own exam date against the official notice.

Can I change the GATE subjects?

Yes. You can add, remove or rename subjects, and the Other option lets you build an exam that is not in the list. If a paper has optional subjects, the setup asks which one is yours.
