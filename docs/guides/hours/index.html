Questions and answers

Does studying more hours always give a better result?

Prepkram makes no such claim. It starts from a default number, you can change it, and the weekly report then compares your planned hours with the hours you really studied.

Where do these hours come from?

They are starting suggestions written from general knowledge, not from any official source. Check the exam body's notice and change the hours to fit your own situation.
